# AMG Aviation Group — Company Overview

A plain-language guide to what AMG is, who it helps, and how it works.

---

## In short

AMG Aviation Group helps aircraft owners find qualified pilots — fast, and
without the guesswork.

An owner has an aircraft but, for a stretch of time, no pilot for it. Their
regular pilot called out sick or went on vacation. Their insurance policy
suddenly requires a second pilot they don't have. Or the aircraft simply needs
to get to a maintenance shop and back, and there's no one to fly it. These gaps
are short — a few days to a few weeks — and there's no easy way to fill them.

AMG fills them. We find the right pilot, make sure that pilot actually meets
what the owner's insurance requires, and coordinate all the details so the
aircraft gets where it needs to go.

We charge one simple coordination fee for that work. Everything else — the
pilot's pay, airline tickets, hotels, fuel — the owner pays directly. We don't
mark anything up and we don't take a cut of anyone else's money.

We serve aircraft owners across Southeast Florida and the Southeast US.

---

## What we do — and what we don't

**What we do:** connect aircraft owners with vetted contract pilots, confirm
those pilots meet the owner's insurance requirements, and handle the
coordination around the flight.

**What we don't do:** we never fly or control the aircraft ourselves. AMG is not
an airline, a charter company, or a flight operator. Under FAA rules, the owner
and the pilot always remain fully in command of the aircraft. We're the people
who make the right connection and keep the details straight — nothing more, and
that boundary is deliberate.

That distinction matters. It's what keeps AMG a clean, straightforward
coordination service rather than something that would require the kind of
licensing an airline needs. The owner is always the one who hires AMG and pays
AMG. We connect and coordinate; the owner and pilot fly.

---

## The problem we solve

Every aircraft owner runs into pilot-availability gaps, and there's no good way
to handle them:

- **The gap is too short to hire for.** Two days to three weeks. Hiring a
  full-time pilot for that makes no sense.
- **The real worry is insurance.** An owner's insurance policy usually spells out
  exactly what a pilot must have — so many hours, so much time in that specific
  aircraft, sometimes a named pilot the insurer has approved. Grab the wrong
  pilot and an owner can accidentally void the coverage on a very expensive
  aircraft without realizing it.
- **Finding someone is a phone tree.** Owners ask their mechanic, who asks a
  friend, who remembers someone. It takes days and turns up one unvetted name.
- **Maintenance trips are a headache.** The aircraft has to reach the shop and
  come home. It's nobody's job, and it holds everything up.

The question owners are really asking isn't "can I find a pilot?" It's **"will
my insurance still cover me if I do?"** That's the question AMG is built to
answer — in writing.

## Referral program

When a maintenance shop, broker, or airport service company refers an owner to
AMG, everyone benefits:

- The **owner** gets **15% off** AMG's coordination fee.
- The **partner** who referred them earns a **15% commission** as a thank-you.

The commission comes out of AMG's own fee — it never adds anything to what the
owner pays, and the partner never has to handle any money. And we always tell
the owner about the arrangement, in writing, right on the quote. No surprises,
no hidden incentives.

## How a job works, start to finish

1. **A request comes in** — from an owner directly, or from a shop or broker
   referring one.
2. **We scope it and quote it.** AMG figures out exactly what's needed and comes
   back with a quote **within 24 hours.**
3. **We source qualified pilots** and check each one against the owner's
   insurance requirements. This is the heart of what we do: no pilot is ever put
   forward unless they meet, in writing, what the policy demands.
4. **The owner chooses the pilot.** AMG confirms the details and documents
   everything for the owner's insurance broker.
5. **The pilot flies.** The owner pays the pilot and vendors directly. AMG's only
   invoice is its coordination fee.
6. **The job closes** with all the paperwork in order.

## How we keep it clean

Trust is the whole business. A few principles hold it together:

- **The owner pays the pilot and vendors directly.** AMG never touches trip
  money and never marks anything up.
- **Our fee is our only charge,** and we state it up front.
- **Insurance requirements are verified in writing** before anyone flies. That's
  the difference between a pilot who's merely available and one the owner's
  coverage will actually accept.
- **Referral commissions are always disclosed** to the owner.
- **We connect and coordinate — we never control the aircraft.** The owner and
  pilot stay fully in charge, start to finish.
