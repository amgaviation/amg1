## 1. Scope of Services

AMG provides coordination and administrative support to Owner in connection with Owner's own Part 91 operation of the Aircraft. AMG's work consists of: identifying and introducing independent contract pilots who hold the certificates, ratings, currency and time-in-type Owner's operation requires; collecting each candidate pilot's certificates, medical, recurrent training records, logged times, W-9 and non-owned aircraft liability certificate; presenting those qualifications to Owner's insurance broker or underwriter and obtaining the broker's written response; assembling the resulting document file for Owner's records; and coordinating schedule, contact information and logistics among Owner, the pilot, and any maintenance facility Owner has engaged.

AMG confirms certificates, currency and time-in-type, and puts the pilot's qualifications in front of Owner's broker for written approval before the flight. AMG does not warrant, guarantee, or vouch for any pilot's skill, judgment, fitness, or performance, and Owner does not rely on AMG for any such assessment.

AMG does not and will not: furnish, own, lease, select, or arrange the Aircraft or any other aircraft; hold out or provide air transportation; act as an air carrier, commercial operator, air charter broker, aircraft management company, or flight dispatch service; exercise any function reserved to the pilot in command; pay, advance, or rebill any cost of operating the Aircraft other than a documented AMG out-of-pocket expense reimbursed at cost under Section 4; or take custody of any funds belonging to Owner or payable to any pilot or vendor. AMG holds no certificate under 14 CFR Part 119, 135, or 295 and none is required for the services described in this Section.

## 2. No Operational Control

Owner at all times retains operational control of the Aircraft within the meaning of 14 CFR § 1.1, and the pilot in command retains the authority and responsibility described in 14 CFR § 91.3. The following are binding rules of conduct on AMG, not merely statements of intent, and AMG's breach of any of them is a material breach of this Agreement:

AMG will not make, direct, approve, veto, or attempt to influence any go/no-go decision. AMG will not make, direct, approve, or attempt to influence any decision as to routing, altitude, alternates, weather, fuel load, fuel stops, payload, loading, duty or rest limits, maintenance airworthiness release, or the release of the Aircraft for flight. AMG will not select, furnish, or recommend a specific aircraft for a trip, and AMG does not maintain any list, roster, or inventory of aircraft available for use. AMG will not represent to any person that lift is or can be made available. If any person asks AMG to obtain an aircraft, AMG will decline and may refer that person to a certificated Part 135 operator, and AMG will accept no fee, commission, or other consideration for that referral from any party.

If a pilot, a maintenance facility, or any other person asks AMG for a decision described in this Section, AMG will direct the question to Owner or to the pilot in command and will document that it did so.

The parties contract and invoice in two separate streams: Owner pays the pilot directly for pilot services under a separate agreement between Owner and the pilot, and Owner pays AMG only the coordination fee under this Agreement. That structure is consistent with, and is evidence of, Owner's retention of operational control. It is not proof of it. Operational control turns on who decides whether, when, and how the flight goes, not on who pays whom, and the conduct rules in this Section — not the invoicing — are what govern.

## 3. The Pilot Is Owner's Independent Contractor

Each pilot introduced by AMG contracts directly with Owner as an independent contractor and is not an employee, agent, servant, partner, joint venturer, or subcontractor of AMG. AMG does not direct or control the manner, method, or means by which any pilot performs, does not set the pilot's rate, and does not supervise the pilot in flight. Owner is solely responsible for negotiating and documenting its own terms with the pilot, including rate, scope, cancellation, and expense reimbursement.

Because Owner, not AMG, is the payer of pilot compensation, AMG issues no Form 1099-NEC to any pilot for trip compensation and makes no withholding, unemployment, or workers' compensation contribution on any pilot's behalf. Owner is responsible for its own information-reporting obligations with respect to amounts it pays pilots. Owner and AMG will each obtain a Form W-9 from any pilot each engages.

## 4. Fee and Payment

Owner will pay AMG a coordination fee in the amount stated on the first page. Fifty percent (50%) of the fee is due on execution of this Agreement and is earned when AMG begins sourcing. The balance is due on the earlier of (i) the date a pilot accepts the engagement or (ii) completion of the engagement.

If the engagement terminates because Owner's broker or underwriter declines to approve any pilot AMG presents, or because Owner elects not to proceed after such a decline, AMG will retain the earned portion and will invoice no balance. The fee is otherwise non-refundable once a pilot has accepted the engagement, whether or not the flight subsequently occurs. Nothing in this paragraph obligates AMG to present more than three candidate pilots. The fee covers AMG's coordination service only. AMG's invoice will state the coordination fee as a single line item, will contain no markup on any third-party cost, and will include no charge stated per flight, per leg, per flight hour, or per mile.

Owner pays the pilot directly. AMG will not receive, hold, escrow, disburse, prepay, advance, or reconcile any trip funds, and there is no pass-through, true-up, deposit, or reconciliation of any amount under this Agreement.

If, at Owner's written request, AMG purchases an airline positioning ticket or similar item for a pilot, AMG will invoice that item as an AMG out-of-pocket expense at documented cost with the receipt attached. Such reimbursement is not pilot compensation and will never be stated as an hourly, per-leg, or per-diem amount.

Invoices are due on receipt unless a different term is stated on the invoice. Amounts unpaid after fifteen (15) days bear interest at 1.5% per month or the maximum rate Florida law allows, whichever is less.

Cancellation, rescheduling, standby, and minimum-day terms as between Owner and the pilot are governed solely by Owner's separate agreement with the pilot, and AMG is not a party to and bears no liability under those terms.

## 5. Delay, Weather, AOG, and Shop Slippage

Delay is an operating risk of Owner's flight, not of AMG's coordination service. If a trip is delayed, extended, interrupted, or cancelled for any reason — including weather, AOG or newly discovered discrepancy, parts delay, maintenance facility schedule slippage, ATC, airport or customs closure, illness, or a special flight permit not being issued or being issued with limitations Owner cannot meet — all resulting pilot day rate, standby time, per diem, lodging, ground transportation, and return-travel charges accrue to Owner's account and are payable by Owner directly to the pilot under Owner's agreement with the pilot. AMG's coordination fee is unaffected by delay and is neither increased nor refunded on account of it.

## 6. Insurance Condition Precedent — No Written Approval, No Flight

Owner represents and warrants, as a condition precedent to AMG placing any pilot and to any flight of the Aircraft under this Agreement, that: (a) Owner maintains hull and aircraft liability insurance on the Aircraft in force on the date of flight; and (b) the specific pilot AMG has identified, named individually, either falls within the open pilot warranty of that policy or has been added to that policy by endorsement, and Owner will not permit that pilot to act as a required crewmember or as an insurer-required additional pilot unless that is true.

Owner will cause its broker or underwriter to confirm subsection (b) in writing by email naming that specific pilot, and Owner will deliver a copy of that email to AMG before the pilot is placed. AMG will not place a pilot and will instruct any pilot it has introduced not to launch until that email is in the trip file. **No email, no flight.** No verbal assurance from Owner, a broker, a producer, or an underwriter substitutes for the written email.

For turbine aircraft, the parties proceed on the assumption that no usable open pilot warranty exists and that an individually named endorsement will be required, with a typical underwriter turnaround of 24 to 72 hours. Owner acknowledges that this approval interval, not AMG's performance, controls the earliest date a pilot can launch.

Where the flight is to be conducted under a special flight permit issued pursuant to 14 CFR § 21.197, the same broker email must also expressly acknowledge the ferry flight under that permit. Owner or Owner's A&P/IA, and not AMG, is the applicant for and signer of the FAA Form 8130-6 application under § 21.199. AMG will not apply for, sign, or hold any special flight permit. A copy of the issued FAA Form 8130-7 and the operating limitations issued with it must be in the trip file before launch, and Owner remains responsible for compliance with those limitations under 14 CFR § 91.9(a).

Owner will notify AMG immediately if any policy is cancelled, non-renewed, or materially amended, or if any approval described in this Section is withdrawn, at any time before the flight is completed.

## 7. Waiver of Subrogation in Favor of AMG

Owner will cause its hull and its aircraft liability insurers to waive, and Owner on its own behalf hereby waives, all rights of subrogation and all rights of recovery against AMG Aviation Group, its owners, officers, employees, and independent contractors, arising out of or in any way connected with the Aircraft, its operation, this Agreement, or any pilot introduced under it, whether the loss is caused in whole or in part by the negligence of AMG or of any person AMG introduced. Owner will deliver to AMG, before any pilot is placed, a certificate of insurance or policy endorsement evidencing that waiver in favor of AMG by name.

Owner will additionally cause AMG Aviation Group to be named as an additional insured on Owner's aircraft liability policy with respect to Owner's operation of the Aircraft, evidenced on the same certificate. Owner's insurance is primary and non-contributory with respect to any insurance AMG maintains, and Owner will cause its insurers to provide AMG thirty (30) days' written notice of cancellation or material change. Failure of Owner's insurer to give that notice does not relieve Owner of any obligation under this Agreement.

Owner will not settle, compromise, or release any claim in a manner that impairs AMG's rights under this Section.

This indemnity applies regardless of the theory of liability asserted and survives completion or termination of this Agreement. This indemnity does not extend to loss caused solely by AMG's own gross negligence or willful misconduct.

Owner will give AMG prompt written notice of any claim to which this Section may apply and will not admit liability on AMG's behalf.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

Neither party is liable to the other for indirect, incidental, special, punitive, or consequential damages, including loss of use of the Aircraft, downtime, hangar or storage charges, diminution in value, lost profits, lost charter or business revenue, the cost of replacement lift or substitute aircraft, or the cost of any alternate transportation, even if advised of the possibility of such damages.

AMG makes no warranty, express or implied, as to any pilot's performance and expressly disclaims the implied warranties of merchantability and fitness for a particular purpose. AMG is not liable for the acts or omissions of any pilot, maintenance facility, fixed base operator, insurer, broker, or government agency.
